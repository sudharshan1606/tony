Our Story

A Legacy of Fashion & Trust

Thineva stands as one of the oldest and most respected dress stores in the historic town of Chidambaram. For generations, we have been serving families with the finest collection of traditional and modern attire.

Our commitment to quality, variety, and customer satisfaction has made us the go-to destination for fashion lovers across the region. We take pride in being a part of countless celebrations, festivals, and special moments in our customers' lives.
